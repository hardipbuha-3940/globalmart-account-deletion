Global Mart - Delete your account

This page explains how to delete your account and personal data for the Global Mart Android app and the online store theglobalmart.store, run by SHIVANTA ENTERPRISES, Gandhinagar, Gujarat, India.

How to request deletion

  1. Call or send a WhatsApp message to +91 7600372321, or use the Contact Us page.
  2. Write: "Please delete my Global Mart account" and give the mobile number you sign in with.
  3. To confirm it is your account, we may ask you to share the one-time password (OTP) sent to that number.
  4. We delete your account within 30 days and confirm to you by the same channel.

In the app you can also open Account → Log out at any time. Logging out removes your sign-in from the phone but does not delete your account.

What is deleted

  • Your account and sign-in mobile number
  • Your name, email address and saved delivery addresses
  • Your cart and wishlist

What is kept, and for how long

Records of orders you placed (invoice, items, amount, delivery address of that order) are kept as long as Indian tax and accounting law requires (usually up to 8 years), and only for that purpose. They are deleted after that period.

We do not store your card or UPI details; payments are processed by the payment provider.

Delete some data without deleting the account

Contact us the same way and tell us which data to remove (for example a saved address). We do it within 30 days.
